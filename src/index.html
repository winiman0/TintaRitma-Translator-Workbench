About Tinta Ritma

Language Services & Education Technology

Founded in 2012 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, Tinta Ritma Sdn Bhd combines "Tinta" (Ink) and "Ritma" (Rhythm)—reflecting our belief that language is both a meticulous written craft and an expressive spoken flow.

With over 85 dedicated staff members, we transcribe classical manuscripts and literary texts obtained directly from authors and archives, building modern technology tools to make literary knowledge interactive and living.

Our Vision

To make literature accessible, living, and relevant in the digital age.

Our Mission

To merge literary heritage with innovative technology so everyone can discover and create language.

Brand Identity & Emblem

The violet hue signifies trust and knowledge, the cursive 'T' stems from "Tinta", and the pen emblem honors our dedication to literature.

Managing Director & Founder: Dr. Huseina Binti Abdullah
Core Team: Software Engineers, Literary Editors, UX/Digitization Specialists & Admin
