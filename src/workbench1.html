Cultural Context & Poetic Analysis Pantun Structure: 4-Line Quatrain

1. Pembayang

"Ten Ringgit is given as a prize, buy a cloak from Batavia's lands..."
Sets up the rhyme and natural imagery. Mentioning Batavia (historical Jakarta) reflects maritime trade and historical commerce in Malay literature.

2. Maksud

"Though we may die, our body dies, good deeds remain in people's hands..."
Delivers the moral lesson: Physical existence is temporary, but Budi (good deeds, virtue, and kindness) leaves an eternal legacy.

Cultural Symbolism & Metaphors

  • Budi: A central concept in Malay philosophy encompassing gratitude, virtue, and noble character.
  • Kain Baju (Cloak/Attire): Symbolizes dignity, protection, and societal respect.
